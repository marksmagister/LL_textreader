LL_textreader is a reading tool for language learners. It stores the texts you choose to import and what you have decided about the words in them. This page says exactly what is kept, why, and how to get rid of it.

There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party scripts on any page. Nothing you read is profiled and nothing is used to train anything.

The legal basis

Performing the service you asked for — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — for your account and your reading. Our legitimate interest in a service that stays up and is not abused, Article 6(1)(f), for server logs and rate limiting.

Who else sees it

Nobody. Your texts and your vocabulary are not shared with other readers, not sold, and not passed to any third party. Two unavoidable exceptions, both structural rather than commercial:

  • Google handles signing in. They learn that you signed in to this application; they do not receive anything you read. Their handling is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • The hosting provider operates the server the data sits on.

If you import a text from a web address, this server fetches that address. The site you named will see a request from the server, not from you.

Where it is kept

On a virtual server in Vienna, Austria, inside the EU. Data is not transferred outside the EU by this application. Signing in involves Google, which is a US company operating under its own transfer safeguards.

How long

Your account and everything in it are kept until you delete them. Delete your account and the rows are deleted — not flagged, not archived. Backups may hold a copy for a short period afterwards before they rotate out. Server logs are kept briefly for fault diagnosis.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, have it deleted, object to processing, or complain to a supervisory authority. Two of those do not require asking anyone, and are buttons in the app:

  • A copy of everything — the account menu offers a zip with every text you imported and your whole vocabulary as JSON.
  • Deletion — the account menu deletes your account and all of its data, immediately.

For anything else, write to the address above. You may also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

Cookies

One, called ll_session. It keeps you signed in, it is HttpOnly and Secure, and it expires after ninety days without use. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to: a strictly necessary sign-in cookie does not need one. A second short-lived cookie exists only during the few seconds of signing in, and is deleted immediately after.
