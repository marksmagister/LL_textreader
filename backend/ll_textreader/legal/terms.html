What this is

A tool for reading texts you bring, in a language you are learning. It colours each word by whether you know it and keeps track of what you have learned. It is a personal project, offered free, with no guarantee that it will keep working or keep existing.

The texts you import are yours to import

This is the important one. You may import text you wrote, text that is out of copyright, text published under a licence that allows it, and text you are otherwise entitled to copy for your own private study. Do not import anything you have no right to copy.

What you import is private to your account. It is not published, not shared with other readers, and not visible to anyone else using this service. The service does not host a shared library and will not become one.

A small number of short starter texts are provided with the app. Those were written for the purpose and belong to the project.

Fair use of the service

It runs on one small server. Please do not:

  • use automated tools to import in bulk, or to hammer any part of it;
  • use the URL import to make the server fetch addresses for purposes other than reading an article;
  • try to reach other people's accounts or data;
  • upload anything unlawful.

There are per-account limits on how often each action can be performed and how much can be stored. They are set well above ordinary reading; if you meet one, you are probably not reading.

Accounts

Signing in needs a Google account, and the number of accounts is capped. There is no other way in, so losing access to your Google account means losing access here — which is exactly why the export exists, and why using it occasionally is a good idea.

You can delete your account at any time from the account menu, and it deletes the data with it. The operator may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms, or shut the service down entirely, with whatever notice is practical.

Takedowns

If you believe something stored here infringes your rights, write to ll_textreader@googlegroups.com with enough detail to identify it and it will be dealt with promptly. Note that imported texts are private to individual accounts and are not published anywhere.
